# Powers, authorisation and production

Shared identification avoids duplicate personal accounts; authorisation remains specific. Access to immunology or cybersecurity does not grant EET-IA access. EET-IA membership does not grant scientific document or repository permissions.

| Act | EET-IA Administrator | AI model evaluator | Initial phase |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Read area editions and records | Yes | Yes | Available |
| Prepare and freeze editions | Yes | Yes | Available |
| Grant or revoke Evaluator | Yes | No | Available |
| Evaluate a candidate execution | Yes | Yes | Blocked |
| Authorise a suitable model | Yes | Yes | Blocked |
| Select an authorised model for production | Yes | Yes | Blocked |

One account may hold these powers under institutional policy. Evaluation, authorisation and selection remain separate acts, retaining responsible accounts, dates and grounds. Granting a role does not authorise a particular execution.

A suitable result for an identified configuration permits consideration of authorisation; it does not grant it. The institution specifies domain, purpose, recipients and validity. Changes to the model, its conditions or documents require determining which evaluations and permissions remain applicable.

Production selection permits one active model per defined institutional scope. Replacement must record cessation of the previous selection and the new decision. Two incompatible active selections in the same scope are forbidden. Performance, costs and provider conditions inform selection; they confer no purchasing or payment authority.

Historical records remain intact. Testing activities do not become production evidence by changing a label. The new administrative area does not reset or erase scientific records.
